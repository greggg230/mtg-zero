# Rules shortcuts: the tracking table (M7e)

Every shortcut the engine documented in ENGINE.md's simplification lists (section 13's
"Deliberate simplifications" and 23.11, 24.7, 25.10, 26.11, 27.13, 28.8, 29.6, 30.7, 31.7,
32.5), one row each, with what became of it. ENGINE.md's lists now point here and keep only what
still applies.

Classes:

* **A**: a rules departure observable in cube play. **Fixed** (M7e part 1, branch `m7e-rules`,
  with a failing-first test in `engine/tests/rules_shortcuts.rs` that names the CR rule; M7e
  part 2, branch `m7e-pay`, the payment model, tests in `engine/tests/rules_payment.rs`).
* **B**: an engineering limit kept on purpose, mostly tied to the frozen action-id layout
  (layout v3: `NUMBER` 0..31, 96 permanent slots, 4 `ACTIVATE` slots, 16 `TRIGGER` slots, 64
  tokens; 32 `STACK` and 32 `EXILE` slots, 64 hand, library-list and graveyard slots: ENGINE.md 58; a graveyard choice's list, 64 cards: ENGINE.md 65.10) or to the search model.
* **C**: rules-equivalent (a representation that offers the same choices or results: definitions
  instead of cards in a search, modes as multisets, dominated options left out) or not reachable
  with the cube's cards.

## Notes on the new mechanisms

* **Event groups** (`Game::ev_seq`, `new_event`): bumped as an instruction of a resolving spell or
  ability ends (`run_frame`), as a resolution begins (`resolve_top`), as a batch begins or ends
  (`begin_batch` / `end_batch`: SBAs, "destroy all", sacrifices, a combat damage step) and as a
  cost starts being paid (`pay_costs`). A "one or more" trigger (`TrigPred::Batch`, "cards put into
  a library") fires once per group. Several instructions of one resolution are separate events;
  one instruction's decisions (Brainstorm's two put-backs, one discard at a time) stay one.
* **616.1** (`replaced_dest`): the results every order of the applicable replacements reaches
  are computed (seven effects, each once); one result applies at once; several make the card wait
  in no zone, revealed (its arrival events held back, and leave triggers that depend on where it
  goes held: N1, ENGINE.md 100.3), and `Task::ChooseReplacement` asks the affected object's
  controller (its owner in a hidden zone or graveyard), right after the move and before anything
  else happens; the card then goes to the library (shuffled), exile, or exile with a void counter
  as the same object. None of these is a self-replacement effect (616.1a).
* **Mox Diamond set aside**: while its controller decides, the card is revealed and in no zone
  (`Zone::Nowhere`, allowed by the invariants only with its pending task). Not given a land, it is
  put into its owner's graveyard from there.
* **Routes** (`cast::route`): codes 0 flashback, 1 Yawgmoth's Will, 2 escape, 3 retrace, 4
  mayhem, 5 Serra Paragon, 6 Lurrus, 7 an effect's permission (alone: the effect offered the card
  that way, a free or cheaper cast is never worse). Two permanents of one kind are offered once.
* **New decision kinds**: `ChooseReplacement` (39) and `ChooseRoute` (40), `NUMBER` ids; 41 kinds
  of the context block's 64 (42 with part 2's `PayPool`). The heuristic bots answer both (`az/bots.rs`: `replacement_choice`,
  `route_choice`).

## The payment rework (M7e part 2)

13.1, 13.2, 28.8.1, 30.7.4, 31.7.1, 31.7.2 and the converge note in 28.8.2 are fixed; 13.12-13.17
are what the new model keeps, with reasons. ENGINE.md 18 and 34 describe the model:

* **Virtual pool**: the pool's mana (artifact-only {C} only for an artifact spell) and the mana of
  the several-mana sources picked so far. `PayMana::vm` is the most of it the payment can spend
  with single-mana sources paying the rest (`most_spent`: every spend of that size tried);
  single-mana picks stop when they and `vm` cover the cost; then the distinct pools the payment
  can leave are computed (`pool_rems`) and `PayPool` asks when there are several.
* **Needs**: a mana ability with a one-mana cost adds that cost to the payment as a `NOT_MADE`
  need, and its mana is `MADE` (`mana.rs::fits`); the matching never pays the one with the other.
* **Card uses** (`Class::res`): a tap source and a sacrifice source of one card combine (each
  card's tap way, sacrifice way and both together are its alternatives in `cands`).
* **Forced sources** (`PayMana::forced`): delve's committed Lotus Petals and the sources an
  "exile n cards from your graveyard" cost needs are activated first; delve's are exiled at the
  end of the payment.
* **New decision kind**: `PayPool` (41; 42 of 64 used), `NUMBER` ids, a minor decision (searched
  like `PayMana`); the bots take its first option (the pool keeping the most types).
